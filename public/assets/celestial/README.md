# Celestial asset

`nasa-lroc-moon-2k.jpg` is the 2K color lunar surface from NASA Scientific Visualization Studio's CGI Moon Kit, derived from Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter data.

- Source page: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720
- Direct source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/lroc_color_2k.jpg
- Publisher: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio
- SHA-256: `F7130A1822681FA7512D7DCFD40DB8C10B9BA4F06777910348698260ED7A2170`

Orbital Vision applies this texture to Cesium's Moon primitive. The Moon and Sun directions are calculated from the current UTC clock; camera distance and framing are cinematic and are explicitly identified as such in the interface.

The lunar inspection camera opens on the illuminated side, rather than reproducing the phase seen by an observer on Earth. Its physical UTC position and NASA LRO surface remain real. A body-centred inertial camera frame preserves zoom and orbit input as the Earth-fixed axes rotate.

The solar inspection surface is a procedural 3D illustration at the current solar ephemeris position. It is not a live SDO image or a claim about current solar activity; the interface identifies it as an illustrative model.
